About Us

Discover the story behind Wright Memorial Event Center

Our Story

Welcome to Wright Memorial Event Center, where every celebration becomes an unforgettable memory.

A Legacy Built from the Land

Wright Memorial Event Center stands as a testament to craftsmanship, dedication, and love for the natural beauty of Eden, North Carolina. What makes our venue truly special is not just its elegant design or versatile spaces, but the remarkable story of how it came to be.

Our buildings were lovingly constructed using timber harvested from the very trees that once stood on this estate. Every beam, every plank carries the history and character of this land. This connection to the property creates an authentic warmth and natural beauty that simply cannot be replicated.

What's more, the entire construction was completed in-house, with care and attention to every detail. This personal touch ensures that our venue isn't just a building—it's a labor of love, crafted to provide the perfect setting for your most precious moments.
