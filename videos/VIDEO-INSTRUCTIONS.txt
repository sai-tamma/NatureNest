HERO VIDEO INSTRUCTIONS
=======================

To add a hero video to your homepage:

1. DOWNLOAD A FREE VIDEO
   - Visit https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/wedding%20venue/
   - Or visit https://pixabay.com/videos/search/event/
   - Search for: "wedding venue", "event space", "elegant venue"
   - Download a video you like

2. VIDEO SPECIFICATIONS
   - Format: MP4 (H.264 codec)
   - Resolution: 1920x1080 (Full HD) recommended
   - Duration: 10-30 seconds (shorter is better for loading)
   - File size: Keep under 10MB if possible
   - Aspect ratio: 16:9

3. OPTIMIZE YOUR VIDEO
   - Use a video compressor like HandBrake (free)
   - Or use online tools like cloudconvert.com
   - Reduce file size without losing too much quality
   - Aim for 5-10MB for best performance

4. ADD TO YOUR WEBSITE
   - Save the video as: hero-background.mp4
   - Place it in this folder (videos/)
   - The homepage will automatically use it

5. ALTERNATIVE: USE AN IMAGE INSTEAD
   - If you prefer a static image instead of video
   - Edit index.html (line ~95)
   - Uncomment the <img> tag line
   - Comment out or remove the <video> tag
   - Add your image to images/homepage/

IMPORTANT NOTES:
- Video will not autoplay on some mobile devices (this is intentional for performance)
- The site will still look great without a video (uses the overlay)
- You can use multiple videos for different pages
- Always use royalty-free or licensed videos

RECOMMENDED FREE VIDEO SITES:
- Pexels.com - Completely free, no attribution required
- Pixabay.com - Free videos, no attribution required
- Coverr.co - Free videos for your homepage
- Videvo.net - Free stock footage
