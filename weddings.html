At Wright Memorial Event Center, we understand that your wedding day is one of the most important days of your life. From intimate ceremonies to grand receptions, we're here to help you create memories that will last a lifetime.

Why Choose Wright Memorial Event Center for Your Wedding

Everything you need for an unforgettable celebration

Versatile Spaces

Choose from our elegant indoor venues—the Ballroom, Maple Cove, and Ivy Ridge—or our picturesque amphitheater. Each space can be customized to reflect your wedding vision and comfortably host both ceremonies and receptions of all sizes, from intimate gatherings to grand celebrations.

Experienced Team

Our dedicated wedding coordinators work with you every step of the way, from your first visit to your last dance. With years of experience creating magical weddings, we ensure every detail is perfect and stress-free.

Exceptional Catering

Delight your guests with customizable menus prepared by our culinary team. From elegant plated dinners to casual buffets, we offer diverse options to suit every taste and dietary requirement, all made with fresh, quality ingredients.

Customizable Décor

Our blank canvas approach allows you to bring your vision to life. Whether you prefer classic elegance, rustic charm, or modern sophistication, our flexible spaces can be transformed to match your unique style and wedding theme.

Picture-Perfect Backdrops

Our beautifully landscaped grounds and elegant interiors provide countless photo opportunities. From romantic sunset shots to candid moments with loved ones, your wedding photos will be as stunning as your special day.

All-Inclusive Packages

Simplify your planning with our comprehensive wedding packages that include everything you need: tables, chairs, linens, sound system, lighting, and more. We also work with trusted local vendors for florals, photography, and entertainment.
